## You may not

- **Target consumers.** Llyner Leads is for business-to-business prospecting. Do not use it to find, profile or contact private individuals as consumers.
- **Harass or spam.** No harassment, threats, deceptive messages or bulk unsolicited messages that break the law. Follow the CAN-SPAM Act for email (identify yourself, include a postal address and an opt-out, honour opt-outs) and never send auto-dialled or pre-recorded calls or texts to mobile numbers without consent (TCPA).
- **Resell raw lead data.** Do not resell, sublicense, publish or share Leads as a list, database or data product. Use them for your own outreach.
- **Discriminate unlawfully.** Do not use the Service to exclude or target people or businesses on a basis that is unlawful discrimination.
- **Break the law or the Service.** No illegal activity, no attempts to bypass limits or security, no scraping or reverse engineering the Service, and no sharing your account.
- **Ignore removal requests.** If a business asks not to be contacted or to be removed, honour it.
