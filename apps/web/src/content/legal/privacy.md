# Privacy Policy

> Draft for founder review. Not legal advice; have a lawyer review before charging customers outside the founder's network.

**Last updated:** 2026-11-19

This policy explains what [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") collects and why when you use Llyner Leads. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 1. What we collect from our customers

- **Account:** your name, email address, business name and a hashed password.
- **Profile:** the information you give us about your business, products and ideal customers, including your chat messages with the assistant.
- **Payments:** purchases are processed by Stripe. We receive the pack bought, the amount and a payment reference; we never see or store your card number.
- **Usage:** which pages you use and what actions you take, to run and improve the Service, including product analytics.
- **Google Analytics:** when we have switched it on, Google Analytics sets cookies to measure traffic (see the Cookie Notice).
- **Technical data:** a session cookie, IP address and basic request logs for security and abuse prevention.

## 2. Business data we collect from public sources, and why

To find Leads for you we collect business information from public sources and data providers: business names, addresses, websites, phone numbers, business email addresses, public descriptions and the names and roles of people at those businesses. We keep the source of each fact and when we retrieved it. We use it only to deliver Leads to our customers and to keep it accurate. This service is for business-to-business use: we do not target consumers.

A business or person can ask us to remove their data from our results. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]; removed entries are kept on a removal list so they are not collected again.

## 3. Who we share data with

We share data only with the providers that run the Service, under their terms:

- Anthropic (AI models that chat with you, draft Plans and assess fit);
- Apify (collecting public web data);
- Google (maps and business listings; Google Analytics when enabled);
- Stripe (payments);
- our email provider (verification, reset and run emails);
- DigitalOcean (hosting and database).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Retention

We keep your account and Leads while your account is open. After you ask us to delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep (for example payment records for tax) and the removal list. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it or delete it by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We may need to confirm it is you. You can stop analytics cookies in your browser settings.

## 6. California notice (CCPA)

If you live in California you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to request deletion and correction, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. To make a request, email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, encrypted secrets and access controls, and we log Staff access to customer accounts. No system is perfectly secure.

## 8. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

## 9. Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date when this policy changes and tell you about material changes by email or in the app.
