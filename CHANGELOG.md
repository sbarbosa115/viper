# Changelog

What's new in Llyner Leads, newest first.

## 2026-11-21: Expenses and P&L (back office, Staff only)
- A new **Expenses** area in the back office keeps every cost of running Llyner Leads in one place: monthly and yearly bills, price changes from a date, and one-off expenses.
- Usage costs from provider calls and estimated Stripe fees are added automatically. Enter each provider's real invoice at month end and any gap over 15% is flagged.
- The **P&L** shows each month's revenue, cost of revenue, gross margin, expenses and net profit, with a 12-month chart, a breakdown by category and CSV export.
- A monthly budget (default $300) emails all Staff at 80% and 100%.
- Refunds now count in the month they happen.
- Customers see no change.
